💻 How It Works

server-api acts as a smart middleman between you and your AI agents. Here's how the magic happens:

  1. You start the server - Run server-api on your computer. It creates a secure local server.
  2. Your devices pair - Your phone, tablet, or other computer connect to the server using a secure pairing code.
  3. You open the web console - Type localhost:3000 (or your computer's IP address) into any browser.
  4. You connect your AI agents - Link Claude Code, Codex, or DeepSeek Harness to server-api with a few clicks.
  5. Control everything - Now you can start, monitor, and manage AI tasks from any paired device.

🔒 Is it secure? Absolutely! All communication between your devices and server-api is encrypted. The pairing process ensures only devices you approve can connect.
